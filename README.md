## About

**CloudSentinel CyberAware** is a free cybersecurity awareness and digital safety education platform by **CloudSentinel Technologies**.

Our goal is simple: help everyday people, students, entrepreneurs, small businesses and organisations understand online threats and protect themselves, using plain language and real examples from Nigeria and across Africa, such as fake bank alerts, OTP theft, SIM swap fraud, WhatsApp takeover and online scams.

## What you will find on the website

- **Learning centre:** easy lessons on passwords, phishing, scams, mobile safety, privacy, data protection, payments and more
- **Quizzes:** test your knowledge and learn from the explanations
- **Videos:** short awareness videos
- **Tips and articles:** practical advice you can use today
- **Chatbot:** quick answers to common cybersecurity questions
- **Languages:** English, Hausa, Igbo and Yorùbá
- **Works on any device:** phone, tablet or computer, in light or dark mode, and it can be installed like an app
